what it does

A real home for the association, not just a ledger

Members directory. // Every registered member, searchable, with leadership roles clearly shown.
Contributions & transparent ledger. // Every contribution and disbursement recorded permanently — no silent edits, ever.
Welfare Support. // Members request help by category (emotional, accommodation, academic, financial, emergency, and more) — the committee assesses and decides, not the requester.
Contribution Drives. // Treasurer-run fundraising campaigns with a live paid/pledged contributor list.
Prayer Group. // An approval-gated space with member-controlled message visibility.
Marketplace. // Members list items for sale; contact happens over WhatsApp, one tap away.
Community Chat. // An open space for the whole association to talk.
Push notifications. // New announcements reach your phone automatically.
permissions and why
Internet, network state // syncs your data with the association's database.
Post notifications // delivers announcements and welfare updates to your phone.
Vibrate, wake lock // reliable notification delivery, even when the phone is idle.
Firebase Cloud Messaging // required by Google's push notification service to receive alerts.
data collection

MESWA stores your name, phone number, and optional profile photo to identify you as a member. Financial and welfare data is protected by role-based access — welfare case details are visible only to you and the elected committee reviewing it, and the ledger never shows who a welfare disbursement went to. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
